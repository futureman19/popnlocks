01

A market with no oracle

A binary market on network difficulty: will consensus difficulty be at or above strike K at the end of the window? Collateral locks into a covenant at deploy. Anyone settles by presenting the real block headers — the covenant verifies linkage, proof-of-work, and difficulty drift in-script and pays the winner.

No reporter. No vote. No dispute window. Forging settlement data requires performing the work being measured.

LOCK

Two sides lock collateral

YES and NO fund a covenant anchored to a past block. The strike is written into the script.

CRANK

Locksmiths turn the ladder

Anyone-callable cranks verify headers chunk by chunk, carrying state in continuation outputs. Each crank earns a bounty.

POP

The winner pops the lock

The final crank reads the endpoint target, compares it to the strike, and the covenant pays out. By itself.

03

Why it exists

Hashrate is the most important unpriced signal in Bitcoin. Miners carry difficulty risk they cannot hedge; everyone else reads hashrate through someone else's dashboard. popnlocks makes the chain the source of its own truth — and pays the keepers who turn the crank.
